The Basic Language of the Web: HTML

Posted by Ud Festus on Monday, June 21st 2027

Hello! I am Ud Festus, a B.Sc holder in Computer Science from Imo State, Nigeria. I am a web developer skilled in HTML and CSS, and I am currently learning JavaScript to become more advanced.

My dream is to become a full-stack developer. I want to build useful websites and applications that solve real problems for people through technology.

This blog is where I will share my learning journey, coding tips, and the projects I build along the way. Thank you for visiting!

My Journey

My journey into tech started with a B.Sc in Computer Science, which gave me a strong foundation. I fell in love with building things on the web, so I focused on HTML and CSS to bring designs to life. Right now, I am in the process of mastering JavaScript to make my websites interactive.

My ultimate goal is to become a Full-Stack Developer. I don't just want to write code - I want to solve problems, build useful products for people in Nigeria and beyond, and create technology that makes life easier.

On this blog, I will be documenting everything I learn: from small HTML tricks to big JavaScript projects. If you are also learning, let's grow together!

Why I Choose web development

I chose web development because it allows me to turn ideas into real products that anyone in the world can use instantly. With just HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, I can build solutions for businesses in Port Harcourt, help people learn, and solve everyday problems.

For me, it's not just about code - it's about creating value and building a career that gives me freedom and impact as a full-stack developer.
